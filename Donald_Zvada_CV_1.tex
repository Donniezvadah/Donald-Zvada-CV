\cvsection{Profile}

Statistician and data scientist working across Bayesian modelling, machine learning and applied
health research. Currently a PhD student at Trinity College Dublin on \textbf{T-DIET}, developing
novel statistical methods for the analysis of longitudinal dietary patterns and their association with
health outcomes. Holds an MSc in Mathematical Sciences from AIMS South Africa and Stellenbosch
University, where a distinction-awarded thesis assessed how large language models solve sequential
decision-making problems, and a BSc Honours in Operations Research and Statistics from NUST
Zimbabwe, graduating top of both department and faculty. Previously a quantitative risk analyst at
EY Zimbabwe, developing and validating IFRS~9 credit risk models.
